This project adapts prose-editing principles for academic rebuttals; it does not reproduce the complete upstream SKILL.md. The academic safeguards and synthetic teaching examples are project-specific additions. They require preservation of facts, numbers, units, formulas, assumptions, uncertainty, citations, source attribution, negative results, and the distinction between completed work, work in progress, plans, and conditional commitments. Technical terminology, genuine disagreements with reviewers, and useful revision references must not be removed merely to satisfy a stylistic pattern. Personal anecdotes, experimental evidence, citations, emotions, or promises must not be invented to create a more conversational voice.

This adaptation is independent. It is not an official Humanizer release and does not imply endorsement by Siqi Chen, the upstream project, or its contributors. It edits for readers; it is not an AI-detector evasion tool and offers no guarantee that text will be judged human-written or that a submission will receive a higher score or be accepted. Applicable conference requirements, including required disclosures of AI use, must remain in force.
